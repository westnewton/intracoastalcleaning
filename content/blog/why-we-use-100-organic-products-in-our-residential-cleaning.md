When we started Intracoastal Cleaning, we had to make a choice about the products we'd bring into our customers' homes. The industry standard is clear: conventional cleaning products get the job done fast and cheap. But we kept coming back to a simple question—would we want these products used in our own homes, around our own families?

The answer was no. So we went a different direction.

## What's Actually in Conventional Cleaning Products?

Most commercial cleaning products contain a mix of synthetic chemicals designed for maximum efficiency. Ammonia, chlorine bleach, phthalates, triclosan—these ingredients work, but they come with trade-offs.

They can irritate skin and respiratory systems. They leave residues on surfaces where your kids play and your pets sleep. They wash down drains and into our local waterways. For a company based on the North Carolina coast, that last part matters.

## The Organic Difference

Our residential cleaning services use only certified organic and plant-based products. That means:

**No harsh fumes.** You won't come home to a chemical smell masquerading as "clean." Our products are naturally scented or fragrance-free.

**Safe surfaces.** No residues that require rinsing or "airing out." Your countertops, floors, and bathrooms are safe to touch immediately.

**Gentle on sensitivities.** Better for households with allergies, asthma, eczema, or chemical sensitivities. Better for babies, elderly family members, and everyone in between.

**Pet-friendly.** Dogs and cats spend their lives close to the floor. We use products that won't harm them if they walk across a freshly cleaned surface.

**Environmentally responsible.** Biodegradable formulas that won't contribute to pollution in the Cape Fear River or Atlantic Ocean.

## Do Organic Products Actually Work?

This is the question we get most often. The short answer: yes.

The longer answer: organic cleaning has come a long way. Plant-based surfactants, enzyme cleaners, and natural disinfectants are now highly effective for everyday cleaning. We've tested and refined our product lineup to ensure we're delivering the same spotless results you'd expect from any professional cleaning service.

Are there edge cases where conventional products might be necessary? Occasionally—and we'll always communicate with you if that's the case. But for routine residential cleaning, our organic products handle everything from kitchen grease to bathroom soap scum.

## A Cleaner Home, A Cleaner Coast

We named our company after the Intracoastal Waterway for a reason. Living on the coast means living connected to the environment. The choices we make—including what we wash down the drain—ripple outward.

Choosing organic cleaning products is a small decision that adds up across hundreds of homes. It's one way we try to take care of this place we all love.
