It's one of the most common questions people have after their first professional cleaning—and it comes up again every December: should you tip your house cleaner, and if so, how much?

There's no single rule. Tipping is a thank-you, not a requirement. But there are some widely used guidelines that can take the guesswork out of it.

## Do You Have to Tip a House Cleaner?

No. Unlike restaurant service, house cleaning prices aren't built around tips. If you don't tip, you haven't done anything wrong.

That said, many people like to tip when a team goes above and beyond, takes on an especially tough job, or has been cleaning their home faithfully for months. It's a way of saying the work was noticed.

## Common Tipping Guidelines

**One-time or occasional cleans.** Many people tip around 15–20% of the cleaning cost, similar to other personal services. For a particularly demanding job—a heavy deep clean, a move-out, or a home after a party—some people tip toward the higher end.

**Recurring service.** Weekly or biweekly clients often don't tip at every visit. Instead, they tip occasionally when something extra was handled, and give a larger holiday tip at the end of the year.

**Holiday tips.** A common guideline is a holiday tip of up to the cost of one regular cleaning, often given in December. If you can't do that, any amount—or a thoughtful note—is appreciated.

**Teams.** If more than one person cleans your home, split the tip so each person receives a share, or hand it to each team member directly.

## How to Give a Tip

- **Cash** is simplest and goes directly to the people who cleaned.
- **Leave it in an envelope** labeled for the cleaning team if you won't be home.
- **Ask the company** if you'd rather add a tip when you pay, and how tips are passed on to the team.

## Thoughtful Alternatives to a Cash Tip

Not every thank-you has to be cash:

- **A handwritten note.** Cleaners rarely get to see a client's reaction. A few sentences about what you noticed goes a long way.
- **A review.** For a local business, a detailed Google review is one of the most valuable things you can give. Mention what the team did well, and by name if you know it.
- **A referral.** Recommending your cleaning team to a friend or neighbor helps more than you might think.
- **Small gifts.** A coffee gift card, snacks, or cold drinks on a hot Wilmington afternoon are always welcome.

## The Bottom Line

You never have to tip. But if your cleaning team makes your life easier, a tip, a note, or a review is a meaningful way to say thanks—especially at the end of the year.
