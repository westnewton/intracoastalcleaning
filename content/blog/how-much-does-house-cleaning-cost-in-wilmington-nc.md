## What Affects the Price of House Cleaning?

**Size of the home.** More bedrooms, bathrooms, and square footage mean more time. Bathrooms and kitchens take the longest, so a home with three full baths costs more to clean than one with a single bath, even at a similar size.

**Condition.** A home that's cleaned regularly takes far less time than one that hasn't had a thorough clean in months. Be honest when you request a quote—it helps us schedule enough time to do the job right.

**Type of clean.** A standard clean covers kitchens, bathrooms, dusting, floors, and general tidying. A deep clean adds the work that builds up over time: inside the oven and refrigerator, baseboards, door frames, ceiling fans, window tracks, and detailed scrubbing of grout and fixtures. That's why deep cleans start higher and are quoted individually.

**Pets and household.** Pet hair, kids, and busy households add up. None of it is a problem—just something we account for.

**Special requests.** Inside cabinets, interior windows throughout, or a garage sweep-out add time to any clean.

## Why Is the First Clean Often a Deep Clean?

If your home hasn't been professionally cleaned recently, a standard clean won't reach the buildup on baseboards, inside appliances, or in grout lines. Most people start with a deep clean to reset the home, then move to regular service to keep it there. After that first reset, each standard visit goes faster.

## Is Professional Cleaning Worth It?

For many households, a professional clean costs less than the value of the time it gives back. Add in a more thorough result and no supplies to buy, and the math gets clearer.
