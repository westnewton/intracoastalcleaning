A clean office does more than look good. It keeps employees healthier, makes a better first impression on clients, and protects the furniture, flooring, and equipment you've invested in.

But "keep the office clean" isn't a plan. Whether you handle cleaning in-house or hire a commercial cleaning company, a clear checklist makes sure nothing slips through the cracks. Here's what to clean, and how often.

## Daily Office Cleaning Tasks

These keep the office functional and presentable every day:

- Empty trash and recycling bins; replace liners
- Clean and sanitize restrooms: toilets, urinals, sinks, mirrors, and floors
- Restock toilet paper, paper towels, and soap
- Wipe break room counters, tables, sinks, and appliance handles
- Wash or load dishes; wipe down the coffee station
- Wipe high-touch surfaces: door handles, light switches, elevator buttons, and shared equipment
- Vacuum high-traffic carpet and spot-mop hard floors
- Tidy the reception area and conference rooms

## Weekly Office Cleaning Tasks

- Vacuum all carpeted areas, including under desks
- Mop all hard floors
- Dust desks, shelves, window sills, and accessible surfaces
- Wipe down desk phones, keyboards, and shared devices (with care for electronics)
- Clean glass doors, partitions, and interior windows at eye level
- Clean inside the microwave and wipe the refrigerator exterior
- Clear out the break room refrigerator

## Monthly Office Cleaning Tasks

- Dust high areas: vents, light fixtures, tops of cabinets, and ceiling corners
- Wipe baseboards and door frames
- Deep clean the refrigerator, microwave, and other appliances
- Clean and disinfect trash and recycling bins
- Vacuum upholstered chairs and furniture
- Scrub restroom grout and tile

## Quarterly and Seasonal Tasks

- Deep carpet cleaning
- Hard floor stripping, waxing, or refinishing
- Exterior window washing
- Upholstery cleaning
- Pressure washing entryways and walkways

## Special Considerations for Wilmington Offices

Coastal businesses face a few extra challenges:

**Humidity.** Restrooms and break rooms are prone to mildew. Weekly attention to grout, under sinks, and exhaust fans prevents musty odors.

**Sand.** Offices near the beaches track in sand constantly, which wears down carpet and scratches hard floors. Entry mats and frequent vacuuming help.

**Pollen.** Each spring, pine pollen coats everything in a yellow film. Plan for extra dusting and entryway cleaning from March through May.

## In-House or Professional?

Some small offices handle daily tidying themselves and bring in professionals for the rest. Others hand off the whole checklist so employees can focus on their jobs.

If you hire a cleaning company, use this checklist to set expectations. Ask for a written scope of work, confirm how often each task happens, and make sure there's a clear point of contact if something gets missed.
