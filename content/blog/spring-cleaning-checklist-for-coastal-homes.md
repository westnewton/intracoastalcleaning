Spring on the Carolina coast arrives early—and so does the pollen. By March, a yellow film of pine pollen covers cars, porches, and window sills across Wilmington, and the windows go up for the first warm days. It's the perfect time to reset your home before summer.

This checklist covers every room, plus the coastal extras most spring cleaning guides miss.

## Before You Start

- **Declutter first.** Cleaning goes faster when surfaces are clear. Set aside donate, recycle, and toss bins.
- **Work top to bottom.** Dust ceiling fans and light fixtures before you vacuum, so debris falls onto floors you haven't cleaned yet.
- **Gather supplies:** microfiber cloths, an extendable duster, a vacuum with attachments, a mop, and gentle all-purpose and glass cleaners.

## Whole-House Tasks

- Dust ceiling fans, light fixtures, and the tops of door frames
- Wipe baseboards, doors, and light switches
- Wash interior windows, sills, and tracks
- Vacuum or wash curtains and dust blinds
- Clean air vents and returns; replace HVAC filters
- Vacuum under and behind furniture
- Test smoke and carbon monoxide detectors

## Kitchen

- Clean inside the refrigerator and freezer; toss expired food
- Clean the oven, stovetop, and range hood filter
- Wipe inside cabinets and drawers
- Descale the coffee maker and kettle
- Clean the dishwasher filter and run a cleaning cycle
- Pull out movable appliances and clean behind them

## Bathrooms

- Scrub grout and check caulk around tubs and showers
- Clean the exhaust fan cover
- Wash or replace the shower curtain and liner
- Clear out the medicine cabinet and drawers
- Wash bath mats and clean behind the toilet

## Bedrooms

- Wash pillows, duvets, and mattress protectors
- Vacuum the mattress, and rotate it if recommended
- Clean under the bed
- Swap winter clothes for summer and clean closet floors and shelves

## Living Areas

- Vacuum upholstery and under cushions
- Spot-clean or deep clean rugs and carpet
- Dust shelves, decor, and electronics
- Wipe remote controls and door handles

## The Coastal Extras

**Pine pollen.** Wipe window sills, sliders, and porch furniture after pollen season peaks, and change HVAC filters more often while it lasts. Keep windows closed on high-pollen days if anyone in the house has allergies.

**Sand.** Vacuum slider tracks and window tracks, then wipe them with a damp cloth. Shake out or wash entry rugs, and add a mat at every door that leads to the beach or the yard.

**Salt air.** A salty film builds up on windows, screens, and outdoor fixtures over winter. Wash exterior glass and wipe down metal hardware to slow corrosion.

**Humidity.** Before summer humidity sets in, check closets, under sinks, and bathrooms for any musty smells. Our guide to [getting rid of a mildew smell](/blog/how-to-get-rid-of-a-mildew-smell-in-your-house) covers what to do.
